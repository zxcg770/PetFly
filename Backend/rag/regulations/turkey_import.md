# Pet Import Regulations — Turkey

## Overview
Turkey's pet import rules are broadly aligned with EU standards. Dogs and cats with complete documentation — microchip, valid rabies vaccination, and an official health certificate — can enter without quarantine. EU pet passports are accepted for EU residents.

## Required Documents
- Microchip certificate
- Rabies vaccination record
- Official health certificate (or EU pet passport for EU residents)
- Tapeworm treatment record (dogs only)

## Microchip Requirements
- Standard: ISO 11784/11785
- The microchip must be implanted before the rabies vaccination is administered
- If the microchip is implanted after vaccination, the vaccination series must be restarted from the beginning

## Vaccination Requirements
- Rabies vaccination is mandatory for all dogs and cats
- The vaccination must be at least 21 days old at time of entry (primary vaccination wait period)
- The vaccination must not be more than 12 months old at time of entry
- Vaccination must be recorded in the health certificate or EU pet passport

## Health Certificate
- For non-EU residents: an official health certificate issued by an accredited veterinarian is required
- The certificate must be issued within 10 days of the travel date
- For EU residents: the EU pet passport is accepted in place of a separate health certificate
- The certificate or passport must include: microchip number, owner details, vaccination dates, and tapeworm treatment record (for dogs)

## Tapeworm Treatment (Dogs Only)
- Required for dogs entering Turkey
- The treatment must be administered between 1 and 5 days before the date of entry — not earlier, not later
- The active ingredient must be praziquantel or an equivalent approved anthelmintic
- The treatment and date must be recorded by a veterinarian in the health certificate or pet passport

## Quarantine Rules
- No quarantine is required if all documentation is complete and valid
- If documentation is missing or incorrect, the animal may be held at the border until paperwork is resolved or returned to the country of origin at the owner's expense

## Number of Pets Per Person
- Passengers may import up to 5 pets per person without a commercial permit
- More than 5 pets requires a commercial import permit

## Airline Policies
Airline pet policies vary widely and are separate from government import regulations — both must be satisfied.
- **Turkish Airlines**: Allows small dogs and cats in the cabin (in an IATA-approved carrier, combined pet + carrier weight up to around 8 kg) on most routes to Turkey, in addition to checked-baggage and cargo options for larger pets.
- **Lufthansa**: Allows small pets in the cabin under the seat, subject to a combined weight limit of around 8 kg, on flights to Turkey. Larger pets travel as checked baggage.
- **Pegasus Airlines**: Allows small pets in the cabin on many routes, subject to carrier size and weight limits; advance notice and booking a pet spot is required as cabin space for pets is limited per flight.
- Always confirm the current cabin/cargo policy, carrier size limits, and any advance booking requirements directly with the airline — policies can change and may differ by route.

## Important Warnings
- The tapeworm treatment timing is strict — 1 to 5 days before entry. A treatment given 6 days before is non-compliant
- Book your vet appointment carefully to hit the 1–5 day window relative to your flight date
- Non-EU residents should ensure their health certificate is endorsed by their national authority if required
